## Why

Three buildings on the street and every one of them the same cream house with
the same red roof. Inside, she can paint a room any of ten colours and lay six
floors; outside, she can do nothing at all — and the outside is what she sees
first every time she opens a world.

A school also has to look like a school from the pavement, or the sign over the
door is the only thing saying so.

## What Changes

- **A designer for the front of a building**: wall colour, roof colour, roof
  shape, door colour and window style, with the building drawn full size beside
  the choices as she makes them.
- **A name over the door**, typed on the phone's own keyboard, the way a book
  is titled.
- **What a building is for** — a house, a school or a shop — which is what puts
  a sign over the door and picks the name it starts with.
- **Getting there**: a paintbrush on the street, on the building it belongs to.
- **A street worth standing in**: a tree, a lamp post, a bench and a post box
  along the pavement, so the space between the buildings is not empty tarmac.

## Impact

- `js/scenes/exterior.js` — the new designer
- `js/render/building.js` — the fronts it draws
- `js/scenes/street.js` — the way in, and the things along the pavement
- `js/model/world.js` — a building's kind and name are hers to set

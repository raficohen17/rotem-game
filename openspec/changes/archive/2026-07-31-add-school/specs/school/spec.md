# school Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Grown-ups and children
A character MUST be a child or a grown-up, and plainly look it, so a class has
somebody in charge of it.

#### Scenario: A teacher among the children
- **WHEN** a grown-up stands next to a child
- **THEN** the grown-up is visibly taller

#### Scenario: Sitting at a desk
- **WHEN** a child and a grown-up each sit on the same chair
- **THEN** both are sitting on the seat rather than floating over it or sunk
  into it

#### Scenario: Coming back tomorrow
- **WHEN** the world is saved and opened again
- **THEN** everybody is the size she made them

### Requirement: Sitting a class down
A room MUST be able to seat everybody in it at once, so a class of six is one
tap rather than twelve.

#### Scenario: A room with desks in it
- **WHEN** she taps sit-everybody in a room with enough desks
- **THEN** each character is sitting at a different desk

#### Scenario: More children than desks
- **WHEN** there are more of them than there are desks
- **THEN** those who found a desk are sitting and the rest are left standing,
  rather than two children on one chair

#### Scenario: Up again
- **WHEN** she taps it a second time
- **THEN** everybody stands up

#### Scenario: Nothing to sit on
- **WHEN** a room has no seats at all
- **THEN** the button is not offered

### Requirement: A hand up
A seated character MUST be able to put a hand up and take it down again.

#### Scenario: Answering
- **WHEN** a seated child is told to put a hand up
- **THEN** her arm is up, and it is visible at the size a room is on the phone

#### Scenario: Called on
- **WHEN** she is told again
- **THEN** the hand goes down

#### Scenario: Standing up
- **WHEN** she gets up from the desk
- **THEN** the hand is down

### Requirement: A playground
A room MUST be able to be outdoors, so a school has somewhere for playtime.

#### Scenario: Painting a room as outdoors
- **WHEN** she picks the sky wall and the grass floor
- **THEN** the room reads as outside rather than as a room painted green

#### Scenario: Things to play on
- **WHEN** she opens the playground drawer
- **THEN** there is a slide, a swing, a sandpit and a ball, and a character can
  use the ones that are for using
